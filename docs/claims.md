# Claim ↔ evidence matrix

Status values: `planned` → `running` → `supported` / `refuted` / `inconclusive`.
Thresholds marked **PROPOSED** must be frozen (status `FROZEN`, with date) before M2 starts; they are never
changed after looking at the data. Full rationale: `00_research_plan.md` §I. Literature positioning and the baselines a claim must beat:
`docs/literature.md`. The faithful KIVI baseline that review required is built and measured (D39).

**Two arms both at zero are not a demonstrated equality.** Several M3 comparisons pair arms that each recorded no
failures; the paired test then has zero discordant samples and cannot discriminate. Those rows state an upper
bound on the difference (rule of three: ~0.6% on 472 trials) rather than claiming the arms match.

Per-metadata-flip hazard: Qwen **1.17% → 2.53%** under pruning (2.17×), Llama **2.63% → 2.55%** (0.97×). **Llama's unpruned hazard already equals Qwen's pruned hazard**, and both converge to ~2.5% once pruned — the shape of a shared ceiling. So the honest reading of the Qwen effect is that **pruning raises the per-flip hazard toward a bound both families share**: a model below the ceiling has something to lose and one already at it does not. Unifies D55 (metadata dominance larger on Llama), D51 (Llama more fragile unprotected) and D57 (Q49 replicating, because a deliberately destroyed token row is a different channel from a uniformly placed metadata flip). **Open, and two candidates now eliminated by measurement.** Dynamic range is ruled out — Llama's values are 500× smaller in MSE at equal SQNR, and a corrupted fp16 scale multiplies by up to 65536 either way, so from a smaller base that is a *smaller* excursion. **Attention spread is ruled out too, with the sign reversed (D60):** the prediction was that Llama attends to more groups, and it attends to **fewer** — effective groups 2.46 against 3.10, top-1 share 0.826 against 0.762, 90% of the mass in 33.8% of groups against 44.1%. On the stated logic that predicts the *lower* hazard; its hazard is 2.2× higher. What survives is the opposite: concentration may raise the **conditional severity** of the flips that land somewhere consequential. D61 builds the measurement that separates that from "more groups are load-bearing" — P(break | which group was corrupted), with the group chosen rather than drawn — and its identifiability check found that the most-attended group is **group 0 in 1.000 of 80 samples with zero position variance**, so the within-family rank reading is confounded with position and the primaries are cross-family instead. **D61's result: the third pre-registered branch, and D60's surviving hypothesis is refuted with power.** Six arms, 31 samples clean in all six, 248 trials per cell. **All four pre-registered contrasts fail D31.** Break rates: Qwen top **2.823%** [1.374, 5.711] / rank 1 3.226% / tail **4.436%** [2.495, 7.767]; Llama top **3.629%** [1.921, 6.752] / rank 1 3.629% / tail **5.645%** [3.392, 9.251]. D60's arithmetic predicted 18.6% and 41.8% at the top cell and **both intervals exclude those by 6.6× and 11.5×**, so the "concentration raises conditional severity" hypothesis is refuted rather than merely unsupported. The point estimates order **inversely** to attention in both families over a 69× range in share (top < rank 1 < tail), six cells and two families the same way — a direction, not a result, since no contrast meets the rule. Hypothesis for it, unmeasured: group 0 is the attention sink, which absorbs mass without its content being read. **Actionable consequence:** an attention-weighted metadata protection policy would be *worse* than uniform, because the highest-attention group is the least damaging cell to corrupt in both families — an attractive refinement of the metadata guard, now closed off by measurement. H2d's uniform guard (+0.075% storage) needs no change. **Still open:** why Llama starts at the ceiling. Cross-family differences at matched cells run the right way (+0.81pp top, +1.21pp tail) but neither is resolved at 31 samples and both are far below the uniform plan's 2.2×. **Three mechanisms now eliminated — dynamic range, attention spread, attention-rank-conditioned severity — and none was the answer.** Power: 31 paired samples resolve 2–4pp, so a smaller residual gradient is not shown absent, only bounded. |

## Vehicle notes

- **RAQ-Probe** is the fault-sensitivity vehicle for H1/Q49 (known token roles, facts inside the cache). Analyses of
  margin changes use samples that are correct at golden; golden accuracy is reported separately.
- **GSM8K is a Level-1 reasoning reference only, not a fault-sensitivity vehicle in M0–M3.** Its cached problem text
  averages ~62 tokens, while most of its KV comes from generated reasoning, which is not transformed until decode-time
  policies exist (M5). Weak GSM8K sensitivity in M1–M3 is expected by construction and must not be read as evidence
  against H1.
- **WikiText cache-conditioned perplexity** measures general LM degradation from the cached prefix.
- **Equal budget means equal *declared* storage, and equal exposure.** An M3 arm's fault model is a bit error rate
  over codes + quantization metadata + its own ECC check bits (D23). Sparing check bits would grant the protected
  arms an immunity no physical memory grants them, so the rate is applied to every bit an arm claims to store, and
  `scripts/report_m3.py` re-checks the budget equality from each run's own recorded budget before reporting any
  equal-budget comparison.
- **Fault plans are replayable only within one layout.** Protected vs unprotected at the same quantization is a
  paired comparison on identical faults; arms with different group sizes or protection maps are matched on rate
  instead (D23 supersedes the unqualified M2 replay rule).
- **The fault rate is a condition, not a claim.** It is chosen by calibration so the comparison has power, and every
  arm comparison is reported at a stated rate. The quantity of interest is the ordering of arms at a fixed rate and
  the rate at which that ordering changes.
- **The unit of analysis is the sample, not the trial (D26).** Trials within a sample are strongly dependent —
  arm D's 12 changed answers came from 3 samples, arm E's 7 from 2 — so pooled-trial tests overstate significance.
  Every claim uses per-sample rates paired over the samples *every* arm answers correctly without faults, with a
  Wilcoxon signed-rank p and a bootstrap CI over samples. Pooled Fisher tables are printed as descriptive only.
  Power therefore comes from **samples**, not from more draws per sample.
- **Noise floor:** in bf16, cache-geometry changes alone move logits by up to ~0.3 (KL ~1e-4) without changing the
  argmax (D10). Effect sizes below that floor are not evidence of anything.
